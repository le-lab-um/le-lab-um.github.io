About

The Language Engineering Lab is organized as a virtual research and engineering group aggregating different projects around focusing on natural language processing, spanning topics such as autoformalization of software requirements, formal and computational ontologies, machine translation, and deontic and normative logics.

The lab is rooted in the Grupo de Lógica e Métodos Formais at the Departamento de Informática, which is in turn part of HASLab a software laboratory associated to INESC TEC, at the Universidade do Minho.

Research Areas

Natural Language Processing

Computational analysis and understanding of natural language text and speech.

Ontologies

Formal knowledge representation for structuring and reasoning over domain knowledge.

Machine Translation

Automated translation between natural languages, from rule-based to neural approaches.

Deontic Logic

Formal logics of obligation, permission, and prohibition applied to normative systems.

Autoformalization of Software Requirements

The process of automatically converting informal software requirements into formal specifications.
